Version: 0.10.0
Date: 2026-11-16
  Features:
    - Ritual: Casper for upgrades. Drag with the Ritual tool to track every built entity in the box that is marked for upgrade. Tracked entities get cyan brackets and map marks; the bar counts them by what they will become. An entity drops off when its upgrade is done or cancelled, and the job ends when the list is empty.
    - Ritual has its own shortcut button and a hotkey slot in Controls (unassigned). Alt-click with Ritual in hand cancels its job.
    - Ritual's bar sits right below Casper's and has the same features: hover/click guide arrow, Shift + click craft, Ctrl + click How many?, Craft all on the icon, Ghost Project, and right-click to change the upgrade target or quality.
    - Job areas: Casper and Ritual remember the boxes dragged for a job (shift-drag adds one) and draw them as one combined outline on the ground and the map, green for Casper and cyan for Ritual. New ghosts (Casper) or upgrade orders (Ritual) placed inside the area later are picked up, whoever on your team places them. Overlapping areas of different players each track the overlap.
    - Ghosts changed with Casper's "Change N ghosts to" move to Ritual until they are built. Undo moves them back to Casper; redo moves them to Ritual again.
    - The Ghost Project covers both trackers. A building needed by both is counted once against what you carry.
  Info:
    - Ritual is part of the built-in Casper, so it is switched off together with it when the standalone Casper mod is installed.
